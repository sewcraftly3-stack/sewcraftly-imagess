About this skirt pattern

A full circle skirt is a favourite with kids because it spins, and this one adds a grown-up detail: a curved, topstitched fly front just like on jeans.

The front is two quarter circles and the back is a half circle, so the skirt hangs evenly all the way round. The waistband lies flat at the front with one button and has elastic in the back, which means it fits for longer as your child grows.

Our sample is sewn in a rust cotton twill and finished with a narrow 5 mm machine hem that follows the curve neatly.

Who is this pattern for?

If you have sewn a few easy projects, this is a friendly way to learn your first fly zipper. The steps are broken down with clear illustrations, and the rest of the skirt is quick.

Recommended fabrics

Cotton poplin, chambray, light denim, twill and linen are all good choices. A fabric with some body shows off the circle shape best.

Sewing tips

Let the skirt hang for a day before hemming so the bias areas can drop, then level the hem. Print the pattern at 100% and check the test square first.
