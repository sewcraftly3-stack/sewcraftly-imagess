About this dress pattern

A classic dress that works for school, play and parties alike. The bodice is fitted with a round neckline finished by a clean bias facing, and small shoulder darts at the back give a neat fit.

The long sleeves are gathered at the cap for a soft puff and stay slim below the elbow, so they are easy to wear under a cardigan. The full skirt is gathered onto the bodice, with an invisible zipper in the left side and a button and thread loop at the back neck.

The pattern has two size groups, Junior with a raised waist and Girls with a natural waist, so the proportions look right at every age.

Who is this pattern for?

If you can sew a straight seam and gather fabric, you can make this dress. The zipper and puff sleeves are explained step by step with illustrations.

Recommended fabrics

Cotton lawn, poplin, voile or needlecord all suit it. A small floral print looks lovely, and a plain cord makes a smart winter dress.

Sewing tips

Use two rows of long basting stitches for the gathers and pull them gently. Insert the zipper before closing the side seams.
