About this vest pattern

A tailored layer you can wear all year: over a shirt in spring, over a fine knit in autumn, or on its own on warmer days.

It has a straight, hip-length body with a one-piece back and no centre-back seam. Side bust darts and back shoulder darts give it shape, and nine small buttons close the front.

Instead of a lining, the neckline, front edges and armholes are finished with interfaced facings, which keeps it quick to sew. Our sample is made in a navy textured wool.

Who is this pattern for?

It is a great beginner project with a polished result. You will practise darts, facings and buttonholes, each shown with clear illustrations.

Recommended fabrics

Medium-weight wovens: wool suiting, tweed, linen, twill or a textured cotton. Avoid very thin or very stretchy fabrics.

Sewing tips

Understitch the facings so they stay inside, and mark the button positions after you try the vest on.
