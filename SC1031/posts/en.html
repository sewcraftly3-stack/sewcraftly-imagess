Sizing guide

Choose your size by your hip measurement in the table below. These pants are close-fitting, with only about 1 cm (3/8″) of ease at the hips. In a woven with stretch, take your size; in a woven with no stretch, or if you are between two sizes, choose one size up. Not sure? Read how to choose your size.

Letter sizes: 00 = XXS · 0/2 = XS · 4/6 = S · 8/10 = M · 12/14 = L · 16 = XL · 18 = 2XL · 20 = 3XL · 22 = 4XL

Body measurements

Finished garment measurements

Waist, hips, thigh, knee and hem opening are full circumferences. Side length is measured without the waistband, which adds 3 cm (1 1/8″). Drafted for a height of 168 cm (5′6″). Seam allowances excluded.

Fabric and notions

Fabric amounts were calculated by laying out the real pattern pieces of the largest size in each group, single layer, plus about 8 % for shrinkage and matching. Sizes 8–10 need only 20 cm of interfacing, as the waistband fits across the width. Size 22 on 115 cm fabric: cut the waistband along the length of the fabric.

Common fit adjustments

Length. The inseam is about 42–46 cm (16 1/2–18″), so the hem sits just below the knee. To lengthen or shorten, cut the front and back across the leg between the knee notch and the hem line, spread or overlap by the same amount and redraw the side edges.

Hips. There is very little ease at the hips. Choose by your hip measurement and, in a fabric without stretch, go one size up.

Materials

  • Main fabric: 1.3 to 1.9 m (1 1/2 to 2 1/8 yd) of 150 cm wide fabric, depending on your size: a medium-weight woven with 2–5 % elastane, such as stretch cotton twill, stretch sateen or stretch gabardine
  • Pocket lining: 0.4 m (1/2 yd) of cotton poplin, voile or pocketing
  • Fusible interfacing: 0.2 to 1.3 m, 90 cm wide, for the waistband
  • One invisible zipper, 20 cm (8″), and one 1 cm (3/8″) button
  • Matching all-purpose thread and an invisible zipper foot (a regular zipper foot also works)

No piece is cut on the fold: the word FOLD on the waistband marks the fold line along its length. Mark the pleat lines, dart circles and the back pocket outline with chalk or tailor tacks.

Design details and fit of the capri pants

The pattern is drafted for medium-weight woven fabrics with a little stretch. The front has two pleats folded toward centre front and slanted pockets with a lining, while the back is shaped with darts hidden under two pointed patch pockets. A straight 3 cm waistband closes at centre back with a button above the invisible zipper. In our photos we made one pair in navy stretch cotton and one in burgundy.

  • High waist with a 3 cm (1 1/8″) straight waistband
  • Two soft pleats on each front, folded toward centre front
  • Slanted front pockets with a pocket bag and lining
  • Two pointed back patch pockets over the back darts
  • Invisible zipper at centre back, closed with a button on the waistband
  • Close, tapered leg finishing just below the knee

The fit is close at the waist and hips, with the pleats giving a little room at the front. The leg narrows gently to a cropped hem of about 54 cm around in size 8, so the pants work just as well with flats and sandals as with ankle boots.

Sewing notes

Fit & style

Close-fitting at the hips, cropped below the knee. Choose by your hips and baste the side seams for a try-on.

Fabrics

Medium-weight wovens with 2–5 % elastane: stretch twill, sateen, gabardine. No knits.

Cutting & sewing

Stay-stitch the pocket and waist edges, use a 70–80 needle and press every seam with a press cloth.

Choosing fabric for capri pants

A stretch cotton twill or stretch sateen with 2 to 5 % elastane is the ideal choice: it moves with you, recovers its shape after sitting and holds a sharp crease down the front leg. Stretch gabardine or a light suiting with elastane makes a more formal pair for the office, and a linen-viscose or Tencel twill with stretch gives a softer, summery drape.

Avoid knits, since the pattern is drafted for wovens, and fabrics with no stretch at all unless you go one size up. Cut the pocket lining from cotton poplin, voile or pocketing so the pockets stay flat under the front.

Sewing steps

Seam allowance 1 cm (3/8″) unless stated otherwise. Sew with right sides together, finish the raw edges after stitching and press each seam as you go.

  1. Front pleats. Fold each pleat on the first line and bring the fold to the second line, toward centre front. Baste across the top within the seam allowance and press only the top few centimetres.
  2. Back darts. Stitch the darts from the waist to the point, tie the threads at the point and press them toward centre back.
  3. Back patch pockets. Hem the top edge, press the sides and the point under and topstitch the pockets on the outline with two rows, covering the dart points.
  4. Slanted pockets. Stitch the lining to the slanted edge, understitch and turn it inside. Place the pocket bag underneath, stitch it to the lining around the curve and baste the pocket at the waist and side.
  5. Centre front seam. Join the fronts from the waist to the end of the crotch curve and reinforce the curve with a second row.
  6. Invisible zipper. Stitch the zipper to the centre back edges down to the ZIP notch, then close the rest of the centre back seam below it.
  7. Side seams and inseam. Sew the side seams through the pocket bags, then sew the inseam in one go from hem to hem, matching the crotch seams.
  8. Waistband and button. Attach the interfaced waistband with the extension on the zipper side, close the ends, stitch in the ditch from the right side and add a buttonhole and button.
  9. Hem. Turn up the 2 cm hem and stitch it by machine or with a blind hem stitch. Give the pants a final press, with a crease down each front leg if you like.

Tips and common mistakes

Pleats facing the wrong way. Both pleats fold toward centre front. Follow the arrows and baste them before anything else.

Gaping pocket opening. Stay-stitch the slanted edge before sewing and understitch the lining, so the edge rolls slightly to the inside.

Bubble at the bottom of the zipper. Start the centre back seam slightly above and 2 mm to the side of the last zipper stitch.

Too tight at the hips. Baste the side seams and try the pants on before the waistband goes on, so you can adjust while it is still easy.

Care

Wash at 30 °C (86 °F), inside out, with similar colours. Do not tumble dry hot, as elastane does not like heat, and press on the wrong side or with a press cloth.

Questions

Is seam allowance included?

Yes: 1 cm (3/8″) on all seams, 1.5 cm (5/8″) on the sides and point of the back pockets, and a 2 cm (3/4″) hem allowance.

Which paper sizes are included?

The free download comes in A4 and US Letter, as a printable PDF with one file per size, XXS to 4XL (US 00–22).

Which size should I choose?

Choose by your hip measurement. The pants are close-fitting: in a fabric without stretch, or between two sizes, take one size up.

Can I use a fabric without stretch?

Yes, but go one size up, as there is only about 1 cm of ease at the hips. Knits are not suitable.

How much fabric do I need?

From 1.3 m (sizes 00–6) to 1.9 m (sizes 8–22) of 150 cm wide fabric, plus 0.4 m of pocket lining and some fusible interfacing. See the fabric table above.

Can I make them full length?

Yes: lengthen the front and back by the same amount between the knee notch and the hem and redraw the side edges.

Are these capri pants good for beginners?

They suit a confident beginner. The slanted pockets and the invisible zipper are the trickiest steps: read them once before you start and watch the video guides included in the tutorial.
