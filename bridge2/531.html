About this princess seam jacket pattern

A smart, structured jacket that works over a dress or with jeans. Armhole princess seams at the front and back shape it close to the body, and a centre back seam gives an even better fit.

The one-piece shirt collar is finished with full-length facings, the two-piece sleeves end with a three-button vent, and two patch pockets sit on the side fronts. The body and sleeves are fully lined, with an ease pleat at the back.

Who is this pattern for?

A rewarding project once you have a few garments behind you. The lining, collar and vents are each shown step by step.

Recommended fabrics

Medium-weight wovens with some body: wool, tweed, linen, twill or denim. Use a smooth lining such as viscose or cupro.

Sewing tips

Interface the collar and facings, and press every princess seam over a ham so the curves stay smooth.
